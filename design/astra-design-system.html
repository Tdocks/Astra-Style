01 — Foundations

Color

Dark mode is the default, not an alternative. The palette is built from near-black, bone, and champagne gold — a stylist's notebook under warm light, never a tech product's dark theme.

Meaning is never encoded by color alone. Every score, state, and verdict carries a numeral or a word alongside its hue.
