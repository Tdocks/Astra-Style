What to judge

  • Hands survived. Full-resolution crops of all six show correct finger counts and no melted joints — the usual generated-fashion tell is absent.
  • Backdrop tone drifts slightly between generations (the tailored frame reads warmer than the saturated-colour frame). Side by side in a pair it is faintly visible. Fixable by pinning a single seed for the backdrop or by normalising the background in post.
  • Faces are cropped in the tile, not in the source — the raw files include the chin and neck. The crop is doing that work, so the cropping rule has to be part of the asset pipeline, not a per-image decision.
  • Cost. Six frames covers three questions. A full quiz at spec §6.9 length needs roughly 16–20, generated once and shipped as static assets — this is not a per-user generation cost.
