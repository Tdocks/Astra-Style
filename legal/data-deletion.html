Deleting your Astra Style account and your data

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Draft for legal review.

This page was drafted from the Astra Style codebase so that it describes what deletion actually does. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer and must not be published in this state. Each [[NEEDS INPUT: …]] marker is an unresolved question.

1. If you are using the guest trial

The trial uses an anonymous Supabase account for server-side profile, wardrobe and styling records. Personal garment photographs remain on your device until identity linking; generated inspiration may be stored privately on our servers. Deleting the app removes local files but does not delete server data. Use the in-app account deletion control before uninstalling.

2. How to request deletion

Open Profile → Privacy & Data → Delete account, review the confirmation and submit the request. The server verifies your current identity and removes owned files and account data. The app shows the returned status; a pending or failed request is not a completed deletion.

Cancel your subscription separately

Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel it in your Apple account settings before or after deletion, or Apple will keep billing you.

3. What deletion removes

Deleting your account removes your authentication identity. Every record in our database that belongs to you is linked to that identity and is removed with it:

Your images

Your photographs and generated images are files in private storage, not database rows, and deleting them is a separate operation from deleting the records that point at them. Deletion is designed to remove everything stored under your account's own storage path — the garment photos, the background-removed versions, the reference selfies and body images, and the Style Studio outputs — and then, as a second line of defence, to sweep any storage record left behind under that path.

The deployed account-deletion server routine removes the owned files through the private Storage API before deleting the authentication identity, then verifies cleanup. Individual image removal also uses server-owned jobs with tracked, retryable file cleanup. Copies you already exported or shared outside the app are not recalled by removing the private origin.

4. What survives, and why

  • A record that a deletion happened. We keep the deletion request record — its status and its timestamps. When the deletion completes, your user identifier is removed from that record and replaced with a one-way SHA-256 hash of it, so we can confirm a specific deletion completed without keeping the identifier.
  • The shared product catalogue. Products, retailers, prices and links are shared across all users and contain nothing personal about you. They are not deleted, because they are not yours.
  • Apple's records. Apple holds your purchase and subscription history under its own policies. We cannot delete those.
  • Backups. [[NEEDS INPUT: backup retention window, and whether and how deletions propagate into backups]]
  • Anything we must keep by law. [[NEEDS INPUT: whether tax, chargeback or fraud-audit obligations require retaining an anonymised billing record after deletion. Copies held by a provider are outside our direct deletion control; this draft does not assert that zero retention has been approved for the account. 5. Deleting less than everything

You can edit your profile and closet, remove individual reference or generated images, and inspect and delete individual style memories. Removing a reference also removes its derived variations and saved memberships. The app refuses the reference-removal request while a related generation is active, so an active job is not partially erased.

6. Getting a copy of your data first

Profile → Privacy & Data provides a shareable JSON export of your account records and safe Storage metadata. Image-file attachments are not included in that structured export. Prepare your copy before requesting account deletion; completed deletion removes the server records used for export. Contact us if you need further assistance.
