# Additional pattern/background validation photos

Eight CC0 photos from https://github.com/alexeygrigorev/clothing-dataset,
selected as the next two non-kids images in T-Shirt, Shirt, Shorts and Outwear
after the original sample. Source URLs, hashes and pre-request annotations are
in manifest.json; the source license is retained. No contributor IDs or people
appear in these selected photographs.

Labels were frozen before requests. Ambiguities recorded at that time include a
small chest graphic (solid/print both accepted) and a multicolored plaid shirt's
dominant color. Neither the acceptance bars nor labels were changed in response
to the model's predictions. Color lexical differences and subjective formality
are therefore reported as their own limitations, rather than silently relabeled.

This sample tests additional pictures from the same dataset. It is not a blind,
independent external review or a random sample of first users' phone photos.
